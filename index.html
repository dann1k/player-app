Player

A personal video player for macOS, iPadOS and tvOS.

Player plays movies and TV shows from the owner's own sources: shared folders on the home network (SMB) and the owner's Google Drive. It is a private hobby project used by its developer and family. It is not sold or distributed through the App Store.

Google Drive

When you sign in with Google, Player asks for read-only access to your Drive (drive.readonly). It uses that access only to list your folders and stream the video files you choose to play. Player never modifies, uploads, deletes or shares your files.

Privacy Policy

Player — личный видеоплеер для Mac, iPad и Apple TV. Проигрывает фильмы и сериалы из домашней сети и с Google Диска владельца. Доступ к Диску — только на чтение, файлы не изменяются и никуда не передаются.
