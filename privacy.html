Privacy Policy

Player · last updated September 27, 2026

Player is a personal video player for macOS, iPadOS and tvOS. This policy explains what the app does with your data, including data from Google accounts.

What data Player accesses

  • Google Drive files (read-only). With your permission (scope drive.readonly), Player lists folders and file names in your Google Drive and reads the video files you choose to play, so it can stream them on your device.
  • Basic account information. Your Google account name and email address, shown in the app so you can tell your accounts apart.
  • Network shares. Addresses and logins of the SMB servers you add yourself.

How the data is used

This data is used only to browse and play your videos on your own devices. Player has no servers of its own. Video is streamed directly from Google or your home server to the app, and it is not uploaded or copied anywhere else. Player never modifies, deletes or shares your files.

Storage

Sign-in credentials (the Google refresh token and SMB passwords) are stored in the Apple Keychain on your devices. If iCloud Keychain is enabled, they sync between your own Apple devices through iCloud. Diagnostic logs stay on the device. They never contain passwords, tokens or file contents.

Sharing

Player does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services. Google user data is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

To sign out of a Google account, use "Sign out" in the app. This also revokes the app's access. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes all its local data.
